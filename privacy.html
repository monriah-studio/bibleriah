Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how the BibleRiah app (Google Cloud project “bibleriah-autopost”) handles information. BibleRiah is operated by Caleb Love (Monriah Studio).

What the app does

The BibleRiah app is a private publishing tool used only by its owner. It uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload videos (and set their titles, descriptions, and disclosure settings) to the owner’s own BibleRiah YouTube channel. It does not access, read, change, or delete any other user’s YouTube account or data, and it is not offered to the public as a sign-in service.

Google user data

  • The only Google account that authorizes the app is the owner’s own account.
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored privately on the owner’s own server, are not shared with anyone, and are used only to upload videos to the BibleRiah channel.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

BibleRiah’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Viewers and website visitors

BibleRiah collects no data about viewers of its videos or visitors to this website. This site uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking. Views, likes, and comments on YouTube are handled by YouTube under Google’s Privacy Policy, and the app does not collect them. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service.

Links

Video descriptions and this site may link to shops such as Gumroad or book retailers. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
