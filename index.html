What we do

BibleRiah posts short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts and similar) that each share one verse from the King James Version of the Bible. Every video shows the verse text and its reference, with calm visuals and gentle music, so you can pause and reflect for a moment in your day.

The KJV text is in the public domain. BibleRiah is run by Caleb Love under Monriah Studio.

AI disclosure

Our videos are AI-assisted: the background visuals and music are made with AI tools, then selected and curated by a person. The Bible verse text itself is quoted directly from the King James Version and is never AI-generated or altered. Each video description carries this same disclosure, and uploads are marked as containing altered or synthetic content where the platform asks for it.
